The first China Forum of Veterinary Cardiology (CFVC 2021) took place in Shanghai from September 6 to September 9, 2021, in a hybrid online and offline format. The event featured leading veterinary experts from China, along with renowned international specialists, including Dr. Claudio Bussadori from Italy and Dr. Christopher Orton from the United States, who delivered insightful lectures.
