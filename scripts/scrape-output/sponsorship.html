Sponsorship
Diamond Sponsor
  • Shanghai Hongyu Medical

    Established in 2019, Shanghai Hongyu Medical Technology Co., Ltd. is a global leader in veterinary cardiology, focusing on the diagnosis and treatment of heart diseases in pets. With its core product, the V-Clamp for minimally invasive mitral valve disease treatment in dogs, Hongyu Medical is expanding into diagnostics, treatment, and monitoring for various cardiac conditions, offering a comprehensive solution for pet heart disease. In addition to developing, manufacturing, and selling veterinary cardiac devices, Hongyu Medical promotes collaboration and knowledge-sharing among veterinarians worldwide by providing training on heart disease diagnosis, treatment, and care for animals.

  • Bangkok Heart Animal Hospital

    Bangkok Heart Animal Hospital is a leading veterinary cardiology center in Bangkok, Thailand, operating three branches across the city. The hospital specializes in comprehensive diagnosis and treatment of heart diseases in pets, including cardiac surgery and 24-hour cardiology care. With a mission to extend the lifespan of animals through advanced heart care, Bangkok Heart provides state-of-the-art diagnostic technology, specialized cardiac treatment, and a multidisciplinary team of veterinary experts (e.g., orthopedic, imaging, dermatology, ophthalmology, etc.). The hospital also promotes knowledge exchange through training programs and collaborations with veterinarians across Asia, North America, and Europe aiming to raise the standard of pet heart care in a sustainable and impactful way. 
  • Thonglor International Pet Hospital

    Thonglor International Pet Hospital is a leading animal hospital in Bangkok, Thailand, offering a comprehensive range of services for pets. Renowned for its advanced medical technology and specialised care, the hospital provides high-quality treatment for a variety of pet needs. From routine check-ups and vaccinations to complex surgeries, cardiology and emergency care, the hospital’s dedicated team of veterinarians and staff are committed to ensuring the health and well-being of pets.
    It operates 24/7 and has various specialized centers, including internal medicine, cardiology, dermatology, ophthalmology, neurology, oncology behavior, dentistry, rehabilitaion, acupuncture and diagnostic imaging.
    The hospital also provides services such as grooming, pet swimming pools, pet hotel, pet park, pet taxi,restaurant  In addition, there are 23 branches in bangkok and surrounding areas, as well as locations in Chiang Mai, Pattaya and Phuket.
    Website: www.thonglorpet.com
  • Shanghai Xinyu Animal Hospital

    Founded in 2019, Shanghai Xinyu Animal (Cardiac Specialty) Hospital is a flagship cardiac specialty hospital under Shanghai Hongyu Medical. As the first veterinary heart hospital in China to perform minimally invasive mitral valve surgery, Xinyu provides specialized cardiac treatments and advanced services for pets nationwide. The hospital also offers training and technical support for global veterinary hospitals and medical centers, drawing on a skilled veterinary and post-care team and state-of-the-art medical equipment. Services include minimally invasive mitral valve regurgitation surgery and hands-on device training for domestic and international experts. By the end of 2024, Xinyu had provided V-Clamp technology training to over 70 hospitals worldwide.
